# CINOVA PROJECT RULES

## 1. CORE BUSINESS OBJECTIVE

This website is a client-acquisition and conversion system, not merely a portfolio or design showcase.

Every major decision must optimize for:

1. Visitor understands their problem.
2. Visitor recognizes Cinova as relevant.
3. Visitor understands Cinova can solve the problem.
4. Visitor sees credible proof.
5. Visitor understands what happens next.
6. Visitor has a compelling next action.
7. Qualified visitors are guided toward booking or purchasing.

The website should make the prospect think:

> "These guys understand my problem."
>
> "They can actually solve it."
>
> "They can handle the wider system too."
>
> "I should talk to them."

---

## 2. CINOVA IS INTENTIONALLY BROAD

Do NOT turn Cinova into a one-service agency.

Do NOT remove or hide legitimate capabilities simply because the service list is broad.

Cinova may provide:

- strategy
- branding
- creative direction
- video
- motion graphics
- graphic design
- content
- videography
- photography
- production
- advertising
- paid media
- websites
- landing pages
- UI/UX
- web development
- mobile development
- analytics
- attribution
- reporting
- testing
- optimization

The problem is NOT breadth.

The problem is presenting the capabilities as an unrelated service catalogue.

Organize the capabilities around client problems, solutions and business outcomes.

---

## 3. CLIENT-FIRST POSITIONING

Never force the visitor to understand Cinova's internal service stack before understanding the value.

Lead with:

- the client's problem
- the desired business change
- the relevant solution
- the proof
- the process

Capabilities should appear as supporting depth.

---

## 4. BROAD CAPABILITIES MUST FEEL CONNECTED

Use the conceptual system:

Strategy
→ Creative
→ Production
→ Digital
→ Distribution
→ Intelligence
→ Optimization

These are connected capabilities, not unrelated services.

The website should communicate that Cinova can own multiple parts of a growth problem.

---

## 5. PRESERVE CINOVA'S VISUAL IDENTITY

Preserve the existing recognizable visual language:

- black / near-black
- white
- grayscale
- neon lime/green
- premium editorial typography
- high contrast
- experimental composition
- sophisticated imagery
- motion
- dark/light transitions
- halftone textures where appropriate

Do NOT replace Cinova with:

- generic SaaS styling
- generic blue gradients
- bland corporate design
- generic agency templates
- stock-photo business websites

The objective is to make the existing aesthetic more commercially effective, not erase it.

---

## 6. DESIGN FOR CLIENTS, NOT DESIGNERS

Visual design must support:

- comprehension
- proof
- trust
- relevance
- action

Do not add decorative interactions merely because they look impressive.

Every major visual section should earn its space.

---

## 7. PROOF MUST BE REAL

Never invent:

- clients
- client logos
- testimonials
- metrics
- revenue
- percentages
- ad spend
- awards
- results
- follower counts
- years of experience
- scarcity
- demand
- deadlines

If real information is unavailable, use an honest placeholder or omit the claim.

Never fabricate credibility.

---

## 8. FOMO MUST BE REAL

Use:

- genuine capacity limits
- genuine audit limits
- real campaign windows
- actual seasonal deadlines
- real production capacity
- legitimate scheduling constraints

Never use fake:

- countdown timers
- "only 2 spots left"
- fake discounts
- fake deadlines
- fake scarcity

Urgency should be credible.

---

## 9. PROGRESSIVE COMMITMENT

Do not force every visitor directly into a sales call.

Support:

curiosity
→ interaction
→ micro-commitment
→ personalized value
→ trust
→ urgency
→ booking
→ offer
→ purchase

---

## 10. DYNAMIC PERSONALIZATION

The architecture must support dynamic solution and industry pages.

Potential route structures:

/solutions/[solution]
/industries/[industry]
/for/[industry]

Examples:

/for/ecommerce
/for/saas
/for/creators
/for/real-estate
/for/hospitality

These pages should use reusable data-driven templates.

---

## 11. DYNAMIC LEAD ATTRIBUTION

Support query parameters such as:

utm_source
utm_medium
utm_campaign
utm_content
ref
industry
solution

Preserve relevant attribution data through the conversion flow whenever practical.

---

## 12. PRIMARY CONVERSION

The primary website action should be a useful diagnostic or assessment, not a generic "free strategy call."

Preferred concepts:

- Growth Diagnosis
- Growth Score
- Creative Audit
- Website Audit
- Ad Audit
- 3 Growth Opportunities

Use the most appropriate context-specific CTA.

---

## 13. CASE STUDIES

Case studies should explain:

- client
- problem
- what changed
- relevant capabilities
- before
- after
- result
- timeframe
- evidence

Use actual evidence whenever available.

---

## 14. INTERNATIONAL TRUST

Do not pretend Cinova has locations, offices, teams or capabilities it does not actually have.

Build credibility through:

- real work
- real proof
- transparent process
- professional communication
- clear engagement structure
- credible case studies

---

## 15. MOBILE IS A PRIMARY EXPERIENCE

Do not treat mobile as an afterthought.

Every major conversion path must work on:

- desktop
- tablet
- mobile

---

## 16. PERFORMANCE MATTERS

The site can remain visually rich, but unnecessary animation must never compromise:

- loading
- responsiveness
- usability
- mobile performance
- accessibility

---

## 17. NEVER OVER-SIMPLIFY THE BRAND

The objective is NOT:

"make Cinova narrower."

The objective is:

"make Cinova's breadth feel relevant and organized around the visitor's problem."

---

## 18. WEBSITE SALES PRINCIPLE

The website should behave like:

PROBLEM
→ PERSONALIZATION
→ SOLUTION
→ PROOF
→ VALUE
→ URGENCY
→ OFFER
→ BOOKING

Not:

HOME
→ SERVICES
→ PORTFOLIO
→ CONTACT

---

## 19. FINAL DECISION RULE

When uncertain between two implementations, prefer the option that:

1. improves client comprehension,
2. increases trust,
3. demonstrates real capability,
4. creates a clearer next action,
5. preserves Cinova's visual identity.

Do not optimize merely for visual novelty.
